About Us

Blessed Providence Green Energy Services is a fully registered company with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria. Since 2020, we have been at the forefront of delivering customized solar energy solutions across Nigeria, serving residential, commercial, agricultural, and institutional clients. Our operations cover key regions including the South-South, South-East, South-West, and North-Central parts of Nigeria, reflecting our strong geographic reach and commitment to nationwide sustainable development.

Leadership and credentials

Our company is led by a COREN-registered engineer, who is also a respected member of the Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE), the Nigerian Institution of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (NIEEE), and the Nigerian Institute of Space Engineers. This expertise ensures each project meets strict professional and technical standards.
